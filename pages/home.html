Biomedical AI · Multi-omics · Health Data Science

Hello, I'm Kira.

I'm a Ph.D. student in Biomedical Informatics at the Department of Health Outcomes and Biomedical Informatics, University of Florida, working with Dr. Rui Yin.

My research centers on robust and interpretable AI methods for understanding health from molecules to populations. I'm broadly interested in machine learning and natural language processing for genomics, multi-omics, and electronic health records, with applications in metabolism, aging, mental health, and health equity.

Previously, I've been fortunate to work with Dr. Luis A. Nunes Amaral at Northwestern University, Dr. Feifan Liu at UMass Chan Medical School, Dr. John Denu at the Wisconsin Institute for Discovery (UW–Madison), Dr. Xiang Gao at Fudan University, and Dr. Xiaolin Hu at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU).
