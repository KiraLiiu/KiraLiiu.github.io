Biomedical AI · Multi-omics · Health Data Science

Hello, I'm Kira.

I'm a Ph.D. student in Biomedical Informatics at the Department of Health Outcomes and Biomedical Informatics, University of Florida, working with Dr. Rui Yin.

My research centers on robust and interpretable AI methods for understanding health from molecules to populations. I'm broadly interested in machine learning and natural language processing for genomics, multi-omics, and electronic health records, with applications in metabolism, aging, mental health, and health equity.

Previously, I worked with Dr. Luis A. Nunes Amaral, Dr. Feifan Liu, Dr. John Denu, Dr. Xiang Gao, and Dr. Xiaolin Hu. I have spent time researching at Northwestern University, UMass Chan Medical School, UW–Madison, the University of Notre Dame, and the Shanghai Institute of Nutrition and Health (Chinese Academy of Sciences), and worked as a clinical nutritionist at Huashan Hospital. I did my undergrad at Shanghai Jiao Tong University in clinical nutrition & public health, and my master's at Northwestern University in quantitative & systems biology.
