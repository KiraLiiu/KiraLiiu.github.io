Biomedical AI · Multi-omics · Health Data Science

Hello, I'm Kira.

I'm Jinwen (Kira) Liu, a Ph.D. student in Biomedical Informatics at the Department of Health Outcomes and Biomedical Informatics, University of Florida.

My research centers on robust, interpretable computational methods for understanding health from molecules to populations. I'm broadly interested in machine learning for biomedical data, clinical informatics, multi-omics integration, and statistical modeling, with applications in mental health and substance use, metabolism and aging, and LGBTQ+ health equity.

Previously, I've been fortunate to work with Luis A. Nunes Amaral at Northwestern University, Feifan Liu at UMass Chan Medical School, John Denu, Dudley Lamming, and Judith Simcox at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, Jeremiah Zartman at the University of Notre Dame, Geng Zong at the Shanghai Institute of Nutrition and Health, Xiang Gao at Fudan University, and Xiaolin Hu at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
