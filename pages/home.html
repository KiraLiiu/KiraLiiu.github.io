Biomedical AI · Multi-omics · Clinical Data

Hello, I'm Kira.

I'm Jinwen (Kira) Liu, a Ph.D. student in Biomedical Informatics at the Department of Health Outcomes and Biomedical Informatics, University of Florida. I work at the intersection of biomedical AI and precision medicine.

I develop robust, interpretable computational methods, such as density-peak clustering and longitudinal multi-omics models, and apply them from the molecule to the patient: deep phenotyping from EHR, and how diet and aging leave lasting metabolic memory.

Questions that excite me:

  1. How can clinical notes and structured EHR data be combined to flag risk earlier and more reliably?
  2. How do we find disease patterns in noisy, high-dimensional data that are both robust and interpretable?
  3. How do diet and aging leave lasting molecular imprints, and what can multi-omics tell us about them?

Beyond research, this site is also home to my gallery, music, and community work.
