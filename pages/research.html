Research

What I work on

Three threads run through my work, from the clinic to the cell.

  1. Clinical AI on EHR

    Multimodal models that combine clinical notes with structured EHR data to predict risk, such as opioid use disorder and suicide risk.

  2. Robust patterns in clinical data

    Clustering and benchmarking methods that find reliable, interpretable disease patterns in noisy, high-dimensional data.

    SimCluster

  3. Multi-omics & metabolism

    Integrating transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics to understand how diet and lifestyle shape lasting metabolic states.
